# PRODUCT.md — Boondock

register: product

## Product purpose

Boondock is a personal, offline-first map for finding where **dispersed (free) camping is legal** on U.S. public land, plus general off-road navigation. It covers the useful core of commercial off-road GPS apps using only free, public-domain federal data (BLM Surface Management Agency boundaries, USFS Motor Vehicle Use Maps, USGS topo). The headline interaction: **tap anywhere → a green/yellow/red verdict** on whether you can likely camp there, with the reasoning shown and an honest "verify current closures" caveat.

## Users

One primary user: an experienced overlander / dispersed camper (the builder himself) who drives forest roads in the western U.S. — WA, OR, ID first. Comfortable reading a topo map. Wants a fast, trustworthy, glanceable answer in the field, often on a phone, often about to lose cell signal. Not a beginner who needs hand-holding; not a fleet of strangers. Single-operator tool, used hard outdoors.

## Theme scene

A solo overlander stopped at a dirt pull-off at dusk, phone in one hand, engine still running, low ambient light, deciding whether the spot ahead is legal to camp before they lose signal and commit to the drive in. The interface must be **dark** (glare at dawn/dusk, preserve night vision, save battery), **high-contrast**, and **glanceable in one second** — the verdict color is the whole point. This forces a dark, instrument-like surface with map content as the hero.

## Brand & tone

Field instrument meets topographic map. Earthy, rugged, quietly confident. Reads like a ruggedized handheld GPS unit, not a consumer app. Copy is plain, direct, and honest about uncertainty — it never overpromises legality. Warm amber as the single action accent (campfire/lantern), forest greens as the surface tint, and **functional** data colors for land agencies (these are data, not decoration).

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: card grids, hero-metric tiles, pill-heavy chrome.
- The signature colors of commercial off-road GPS apps; do not copy another brand.
- Neon-on-black "crypto/gamer" dark mode. This is dark because it's used outdoors at night, not for edginess.
- Glassmorphism everywhere, gradient text, decorative blur.

## Strategic principles

1. **The map is the product.** Chrome recedes; the map and the verdict color dominate. No element competes with map legibility.
2. **One-second verdict.** Color-first (green/yellow/red), then title, then reasoning. A user mid-drive should get the answer from the color alone.
3. **Honest by construction.** Never imply certainty we don't have. Closures/orders aren't in any feed, so every verdict surfaces that limit.
4. **Glove-friendly, sunlight-and-dusk-friendly.** Big tap targets, strong contrast, tabular numerals for coordinates.
5. **Free and offline.** No keys, no subscription, works with no signal once a region is downloaded.
